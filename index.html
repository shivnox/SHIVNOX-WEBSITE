What types of workflows can SHIVNOX automate?

Potential applications include lead management, data processing, document handling, reporting, and routine communication workflows. Suitability depends on your process, existing tools, and requirements.

Not necessarily. We first evaluate your current tools and determine whether a practical integration or workflow automation is feasible.

How much does an automation project cost?

Focused projects start at $199. The final price depends on the agreed scope, complexity, integrations, and testing requirements.

How long does implementation take?

Timing depends on the workflow, access, integrations, and testing requirements. We confirm a realistic timeline after understanding the scope.

Automation can include human review and approval points, especially for uncertain, sensitive, or consequential outputs. The design depends on the process.

Documentation, monitoring, maintenance, hosting, third-party usage costs, and further improvements are discussed according to the project and agreed separately.
